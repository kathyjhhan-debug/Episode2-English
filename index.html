1. RA Chapter: Strategic Coordinator of Cross-functional Collaboration and Regulatory Innovation

Based on robust regulatory strategies, RA protects the value of products throughout their entire lifecycle and closely collaborates with various teams to drive the successful market entry of new drugs.

  • Leading New Drug Approvals & Navigating the 240-Day Review System: We lead the approval process by joining forces with global and local teams (Medical, QC, PtS, EG, Squad, etc.) to ensure accelerated patient access to innovative new drugs. In particular, in line with the MFDS's '240-day new drug review system', we are thoroughly preparing a company-wide 'One-Team' response to successfully meet our targets without delay despite aggressive timelines.
  • Comprehensive Lifecycle Management to Protect Product Competitiveness: Collaboration continues seamlessly before and after approval. Together with QA, we ensure that approved product information is accurately reflected on the labeling, and coordinate import schedules with SCM to support a stable supply. We also support QC's Analytical Method Transfer (AMT) and firmly defend market exclusivity through the patent linkage system and regulatory data protection (RDP).
  • Preemptive Innovation to Accelerate Future New Drug Launches: Led by Roche RA, we successfully completed the first global joint review (EMA OPEN) with the MFDS, contributing to the stable supply of Roche products and regulatory harmonization. Furthermore, by achieving the first-ever Real-Time Release Testing (RTRT) approval for a biological product in Korea, we preemptively lowered the barrier for subsequent new drug launches, and built an AI-powered deficiency response database to further enhance efficiency and predictability in regulatory responses.

💡 [Key Message from the RA Chapter]

RA is not simply an administrative submission function. We are strategic coordinators responsible for the lifecycle value of products, designing optimal regulatory roadmaps and leading collaboration from NDA preparation to post-marketing phases.

2. QC Chapter: Uncompromising Quality Connecting the Journey of Medicines

QC is more than just a laboratory; it serves as a robust quality hub connecting global manufacturing sites, Roche Korea, SCM, and QA, safeguarding business continuity.

  • A Vital Bridge to Product Release, from SCM to QA: Upon receiving inbound shipments and testing requests from SCM, we promptly conduct tests and approve the Certificate of Analysis (CoA), ultimately enabling QA to release products in compliance with regulations. This year, we contributed to the 100% on-time supply of all medicines, including highly complex biopharmaceuticals, without a single disruption.
  • Safeguarding the Supply Chain alongside SCM: Whenever global supply issues arise, we hold bi-weekly/monthly coordination meetings with SCM. Through this, we flexibly readjust the priorities of batch testing and release schedules and safeguard industry-leading analysis lead times (25 days for large molecules, 10 days for small molecules), helping to sustain business operations without interrupting the supply of essential medicines.
  • Proactive Preparation for New Launches (Behind the Launch): When introducing new products, we preemptively initiate thorough specification & test method reviews and operate a fast track starting a full year ahead of import. By transferring (AMT) the standard analytical methods of the Swiss headquarters to the Hanam Biocenter, we lay the foundation for commercial squads and disease teams to introduce products to the market on schedule.

💡 [Key Message from the QC Chapter]

QC is not just about reporting test numbers. We are the front line of business continuity, safely connecting global quality to Korea through uncompromising standards and data integrity.

3. QA Chapter: Building an Ecosystem Centered on Product Release

QA is not simply a chapter that checks regulations. Until finished products reach the Korean market and patients, we organically coordinate internal and external partners and defend against risks under the non-negotiable top priority value of patient safety.

  • Company-wide Logistics and Warehouse Risk Mitigation: When import volumes surged by 200% due to the introduction of the FHLR's implementation of the new system (ASPIRE), QA collaborated with SCM to swiftly complete the verification test before using the additional cold storage warehouse. Please be sure to check out the vivid scene of the Anseong Zuellig warehouse, protected in unison with SCM, through the Warehouse Video (Click!)!
  • Thorough On-site Audits of External Vendors: We conduct regular inspections and on-site audits of Kongsung (handling storage during customs clearance and transport to the warehouse), ZPK (the warehouse managing overall product logistics), and Mookoongwha (manufacturing redressing materials). Through this, we manage to maintain Roche's flawless quality standards at exactly 100% even among external partners.
  • Joint Response to Customer Complaints: When complaints are received from the market, we collaborate with PJP, Medical, VC, and global investigation sites to identify the cause, and prevent customer complaints from escalating into a company brand crisis through prompt follow-up actions.

💡 [Key Message from the QA Chapter]

At Roche, Quality is the Right of every Patient and Customer and the Responsibility of Every Employee. The release approval button pressed by QA every day is not a simple procedure; it is Roche's firm promise to protect patient safety with perfect quality.

4. MA Chapter: Strategic Pioneer Opening Paths for Patient Access

Based on sophisticated reimbursement strategies that prove product value, MA closely communicates with the government and stakeholders, pioneering the path to expand patient access to innovative new drugs.

  • Establishing Value-Based Optimal Market Access Strategies: Through communication with relevant internal departments, we derive the clinical and economic value of innovative new drugs, and based on this, design a customized reimbursement listing roadmap that maximizes product value.
  • New Drug Listing and Post-listing Management through Strategic Communication: We fiercely persuade government and agencies such as HIRA and NHIS to lead new listings and reimbursement expansions, increasing patients' access to treatment and supporting the sustainable growth of Roche Korea. Following listing, we proactively mitigate price reduction risks associated with Price-Volume Agreement (PVA) and Actual Transaction Price (ATP) by executing a comprehensive post-marketing management strategy.
  • Agile Response to Healthcare Regulations and Drug Pricing Policy Changes: While driving policy changes across the healthcare system to ensure the value of our medicines is fully recognized, we continuously monitor the rapidly changing regulatory environment and government policy trends, such as the implementation of the Drug Flexible Pricing (DFP) and the re-evaluation of listed drugs, to preemptively predict risk factors and opportunities and respond flexibly.

💡 [Key Message from the MA Chapter]

MA is the frontline pioneer of value demonstration, transforming the clinical value of innovative new drugs into practical treatment opportunities for patients by overcoming complex regulatory barriers.
